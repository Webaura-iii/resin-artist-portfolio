About the Artist

Noor

Hi, I’m Noor, a resin artist who loves turning simple ideas into beautiful handmade pieces. What started as a creative interest slowly became a passion for working with resin. I enjoy experimenting with colours, textures, flowers, and tiny details to create pieces that feel personal and unique. From delicate earrings and charming keychains to custom handmade creations, every piece is carefully designed and made with patience. Since each item is handmade, no two pieces are ever exactly the same — and that is what makes them special. For me, resin art is not just about creating something beautiful. It is about adding a little creativity to everyday life and making pieces that people can connect with, gift, and keep close. Thank you for visiting my little creative space. I hope you find something here that feels special to you.
